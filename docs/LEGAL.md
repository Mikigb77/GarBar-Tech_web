# Company website legal pages

The owner requested privacy and terms based on TaskFlow on 8 November 2026. The starting source
is `TaskFlowWeb/src/content/legal/en.ts` (and its Spanish and Catalan counterparts), adapted to
the company's informational website. `src/content/policies.ts` is the aligned English, Spanish
and Catalan source. Registered company facts and contact details come from `src/content/site.ts`.

## Scope and factual limits

The company site provides information, product links and email contact. It has no product accounts,
checkout, app database, recording, AI submission, analytics or advertising integration. It uses URL
languages, local fonts and a user-initiated clipboard button. Do not copy TaskFlow's subscription,
voice, Neon, RevenueCat, Turnstile or app-account clauses into this site's notice.

The private publication uses OpenAI Sites, its sign-in gate and Cloudflare delivery infrastructure.
Google Workspace is the company email provider documented in TaskFlow's company/legal sources.
The notice identifies those functions without claiming that all providers are EU-only or that
GarBar receives ChatGPT passwords. Product policies and support remain on their product sites.

TaskFlow's 30-day application-log setting is not evidence of a retention setting for this site's
hosting platform. The company notice therefore explains retention criteria and gives a contact
route for applicable provider periods, safeguards and exceptions. Confirm the final hosting and
email arrangements when moving the company website to another provider or a public company domain.

The terms cover company-site access, content rights, responsible use, product status, availability,
complaints, applicable law and changes. They create no subscriptions and preserve mandatory rights.
